Answer

This page is for participants who were asked to answer. No login or account is needed.

Open the answer page

  1. Press [Answer] on the poll message in the channel.
  2. A link to the answer page arrives in a message only you can see.
  3. Press the link to open the answer page in your browser.

The link is yours alone and can be used once within 10 minutes. Don't share it with anyone. To open the page again, press [Answer] again.

Enter your availability

  • For each option, choose with the buttons such as ○, △ and × (the choices can differ between polls).
  • [All ○] and [All ×] give every option the same answer at once.
  • Add a note in "Comment" (optional, up to 100 characters), for example "I can join from 8:30 pm".
  • Finally, press [Save]. Your answers aren't sent until you save. After saving, the "Your answer has been saved" page opens. Press [View results] to open the results page with everyone's answers in a table.

See other people's answers

  • If no one has answered yet, "You are the first to answer." is shown at the top of the page.
  • The current counts are shown under each option's time. The best option (★) is shown on the results page.
  • Open [Everyone's answers] at the bottom of the page to see a table of who chose what for each option ("—" for options not answered yet).
  • Counts are based on saved answers. Press [↻ Refresh] to see the latest.
  • [View results] opens the results page with everyone's answers in a table.

Your answers and comment are shown to all participants and the organizer of this poll. People who aren't participants can't see them.

Change your answer

  • While the poll exists, you can change your answer at any time, even after the deadline or after a date is confirmed.
  • Open the answer page again from [Answer] on the poll message, change your answers and save.
  • If you change your answer after a date is confirmed, it is shown as "Changed after confirmation" on the organizer's results page.

Display time zone and language

Dates and times are shown in your browser's time zone. The time zone in use is stated just above the list of options, for example "All times are shown in Japan Standard Time (UTC+9)." You can change it in the field at the top right. Type in the field to filter the list (e.g. Tokyo, Japan, +9). The page language follows your browser's language, and you can switch it with [日本語] / [English] at the top right. Your choices are stored only in your browser.
