Publish

Deadline

  • The deadline is a guide for "please answer by this date". If you leave it empty, it is 7 days after publishing.
  • Participants can still enter and change answers after the deadline. The deadline is used for reminders and for what the pages show.

Publish

  1. Enter a title and at least one date option.
  2. Press [Publish and post to the channel].
  3. The "Published" page opens, and a poll message is posted to the channel.
  4. An announcement is then posted as a reply to the poll message. It mentions the role if participants were chosen by role, or @everyone if not.

For mentions to notify people, the bot needs the "Mention @everyone, @here, and All Roles" permission (for a role, turning on "Allow anyone to @mention this role" in the role's settings also works). Without it, the mention is shown but no one is notified. Reposting to the channel doesn't mention anyone.

If the button can't be pressed, the missing items are shown in red below it, for example "Enter title."

The poll message

The poll message posted to the channel shows the number of "available" answers for each option and how many have answered, with these buttons:

ButtonWhat it does
AnswerSends the participant who pressed it a personal link to the answer page.
View resultsSends a personal link to the results page.
HelpShows a short guide.

The channel doesn't show who chose what. Individual answers can be seen only by participants on the web pages.

What you can change after publishing

  • The title, description, deadline, date options, participants and answer choices can be changed after publishing on the edit page (open it from [Manage my polls] or [Edit the poll] on the results page).
  • After publishing, the title can't be left empty, and the way participants are chosen can't be changed.
  • Changes are reflected in the poll message too.
