Change the answer choices

By default there are three answer choices: ○ Yes, △ Maybe and × No. Use [Change] under "Answer choices" on the edit page to change which choices there are and how many.

Templates

TemplateChoices
Standard (default)○ Yes, △ Maybe, × No
Yes / No○ Yes, × No
4 levels◎ Definitely, ○ Yes, △ Maybe, × No
With time notes○ Yes, L Late, E Early exit, × No

The templates are shown in the language of the edit page (in Japanese, for example, the standard template is ○ 参加, △ 未定, × 不可).

Make your own

Starting from a template, you can edit, add and delete choices with the items below. A poll has 2 to 5 choices.

ItemDetails
Symbol1–2 characters shown on the buttons and in the table (e.g. ◎ ○ △ × ?)
NameWhat the symbol means (1–10 characters, unique within the poll)
TypeAvailable / Maybe / Not available. Used for the counts, for picking the best option (★) and for the channel display. At least one "Available" and one "Not available" choice are required.
ScoreThe weight used to pick the best option (0–10). It's filled in when you choose a type (Available 4, Maybe 2, Not available 0).
ColorChoose from a fixed set of colors.

Changing them after publishing

  • You can change the symbol, name, color, order, type and score, and add choices, even when there are answers. Participants who answered earlier see "The organizer changed the answer choices".
  • When you delete a choice that has answers, choose which choice to move those answers to.
  • After the deadline or after a date is confirmed, the choices can't be changed (so that the meaning of answers doesn't change partway).
