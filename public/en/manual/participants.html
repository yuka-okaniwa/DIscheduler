Choose participants

Choose the participants under "Participants" on the edit page. Only participants can answer and see the other participants' answers.

How participants are chosen

MethodParticipants
Automatic (channel)Members who can see the channel where the command was run
Automatic (role)Members of the role chosen under "Role"
Choose manuallyStarts with only the organizer; add people by searching for their names
  • With any method, bot accounts are never added as participants.
  • Changing the method or the role resets the participants (people you added or removed by hand are undone).
  • The method can't be changed after publishing. You can still add and remove participants.
  • A poll can have up to 100 participants. If there would be more, narrow it down with a role.

We recommend choosing participants with "Automatic" and a role. Then only that role's members are notified when the poll is published and in event reminders. Without a role, everyone in the channel is notified with @everyone.

Add and remove participants

  • Type a name in "Add members", press [Search] and add the person from the results.
  • Remove someone with [Delete] in the participant list. They can no longer open the poll's pages from that moment.

When a non-participant presses [Answer]

  • They can't answer (default): they see "You're not a participant in this poll".
  • Anyone who can see the channel is added as a participant and can answer: if the person is a server member who can see the poll message's channel, they are added as a participant and can answer.

When participants aren't added automatically

If the bot's Server Members Intent isn't available, participants can't be collected automatically. The poll then switches to "Choose manually" and the page tells you so. Search for people by name and add them.
