Security policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

DIscheduler is a scheduling bot for Discord servers. It handles personal information (participants' schedules), so security and privacy come first in its design.

It does not rely on hiding how it works: all of its safeguards are public, and only keys and tokens are kept secret. The design details (threat model, safeguards and remaining risks) are published in the security design document (in Japanese).

Summary

  • Answers are shared only among the participants of a poll and are not visible to anyone else.
  • No password or login is required. Answer links are delivered in messages only the recipient can see.
  • Only the data needed for scheduling is stored, and it is deleted automatically 6 months after the deadline or confirmation.
  • The bot works with only the permissions it needs, without the Administrator permission.

What this app does not do

  • It doesn't ask for your Discord password or an OAuth login
  • It doesn't request the Administrator permission
  • It doesn't read message content (the Message Content Intent is not used)
  • It doesn't read message history from before it was added either (it doesn't ask for the Read Message History permission and has no code that fetches channel messages)
  • It doesn't store email addresses, IP addresses or avatar images
  • It doesn't mention @here. It mentions the role used to choose the participants (or @everyone without one) only when a poll is published, when dates are announced, and in event reminders (mentions in date announcements are limited to 3 per poll per hour)
  • It doesn't load outside analytics, ads or CDNs
  • It doesn't load outside analytics or CDNs. Ads are shown only on the home page, this page and the answer-saved page, inside a frame isolated from the site, and you can decline them (they never appear on the create, answer or results pages or in Discord messages. See About ads)
  • The donation link is a plain link to an outside service; no scripts or images from the support service are loaded

Who can see answers

Each person's answers (○△× and their comment) are shared among the participants of that poll so that everyone can find a time, and are not visible to non-participants (outsiders or members of the same server who are not participants).

Who / whereWhat is visible
Participants and the organizer of the pollEveryone's answers and comments
The poll message in the channelOnly the number of ○ for each option and how many have answered
Date announcements and Discord eventsOnly the confirmed dates and a mention of the participants. Events are visible only within the server
Non-participants and people outside the serverNothing (even if they know the poll's URL)

There are no public view links or export features. Someone removed from the participants can no longer open the pages from that moment. There is no viewing screen for the operator either, and answers are never written to logs.

Data stored and retention

Poll title, description and date optionsSame as above
Each participant's answers (○△×) and commentSame as above (shown to all participants of the same poll)

Your display time zone and language settings are stored only in your browser and are not sent to the server.

Organizers can delete a poll immediately at any time from "My polls", opened with [Manage my polls] in /schedule. Removing the bot from a server also deletes that server's data.

When participants are set automatically

With automatic participants (the default), the server's member list (user IDs, display names and roles) is fetched from Discord. Only the information of people who become participants is stored; nothing about anyone else is kept. Organizers can also switch to choosing participants by hand for each poll.

Main safeguards

AreaSafeguard
Discord integrationEvery request is verified with its Ed25519 signature, and replay attacks are prevented
IdentityAnswer links valid for 10 minutes and usable once are issued in Discord messages only the recipient can see
SessionsHttpOnly / Secure / SameSite=Strict cookies that expire after 12 hours and are valid for a single poll (an organizer's "My polls" uses a separate session limited to the polls they organize in that server)
Web attacksStrict CSP, CSRF tokens, Origin checks, prepared statements and XSS protection
Access controlAnswers are shared only among participants. Only the organizer can confirm or delete
Data minimizationOnly the minimum data is stored, and it is permanently deleted 6 months after the deadline or confirmation
AvailabilityCloudflare DDoS protection, rate limits (including limits on creating polls) and input size limits
DevelopmentMinimal dependencies, Dependabot, CodeQL, secret scanning, branch protection and SHA-pinned GitHub Actions

Public pages, including this one, are served with the same strict CSP as the app. No outside scripts, fonts or images are loaded.

Where commands can be used

Server admins choose the channels where commands work in Discord's built-in Integrations settings, and who can use them in a channel follows that channel's permissions (both are enforced by Discord). Commands from channels that aren't allowed, or from people without permission, never reach the bot.

To prevent abuse, the number of polls is limited (10 per person per hour, 100 per server per day, and 20 active polls per channel).

Remaining risks

These are points that can't be fully prevented, or trade-offs chosen for convenience.

ItemDetails and response
Forwarded answer linksIf a participant gives their answer link to someone else, that person can view and answer as them once within 10 minutes. Links are single use, valid for 10 minutes and for one poll only, which limits the impact. Please don't share your answer links.
RetentionSchedule data remains for 6 months after the deadline or confirmation, so it can be used for looking back or rescheduling. Organizers can delete it at any time.
Commands right after adding the botRight after the bot is added, anyone who can send messages can use the commands in every channel. To limit where they work, an admin should choose the channels in the Integrations settings.
Cloudflare and DiscordData is stored on Cloudflare and exchanged through Discord. Their infrastructure or staff could access it; this depends on each company's policies. Storing as little data as possible keeps the impact small.
Free planThere is no uptime guarantee (SLA). On busy days that go over Cloudflare's free tier, the service is unavailable until 00:00 UTC. This costs nothing, and answers already entered are not lost.
AdsInside the ad frame, the ad network's scripts run and its cookies are used. Inappropriate ads, or ads leading to malicious sites, can't be ruled out entirely. The frame is isolated from the site, so ads can't read schedules or answers. You can decline ads with the button below the frame. If you see a problematic ad, please tell us through Reporting a vulnerability.
Encryption at restStored data is encrypted by Cloudflare. The app adds no extra encryption: it stores only schedules and display names, and complicating key management was judged to be the bigger risk.

About ads

To cover running costs, ads are shown only on the home page, this page and the answer-saved page. Ads are served by Ninja AdMax.

  • Where: only in the frame labeled "Advertisement" on the home page, this page and the page shown after saving an answer. The answer-saved page doesn't show answers, names or the poll title, and no information about which poll was answered is sent to our server or the ad network. Ads never appear on the create, answer, results or confirmation pages, or in messages posted to Discord.
  • Isolation from the site: the ad frame is loaded from a separate address (ads.etlits.dev). Browser security keeps the ad scripts from touching this site's pages, cookies or stored data, and ads can't navigate this page to another site. Schedules, answers and names are never passed to the ad frame.
  • Ad network cookies and tracking: when ads are shown, your IP address, browser information and the ad frame's address are sent to the ad network. The ad network may record browsing patterns with cookies and similar means to show relevant ads; this is governed by the ad network's privacy policy.
  • How to decline: press "Decline ads and the ad network's cookies" below the ad frame. Ads then stop loading and nothing is sent to the ad network. Your choice is stored only in your browser, not on our server. If Global Privacy Control (a "do not track" setting) is on in your browser, ads start out declined. Declining ads doesn't affect the bot's features.

Reporting a vulnerability

If you find a vulnerability, please don't open a public issue. Report it through GitHub's private vulnerability reporting (the repository's Security tab → "Report a vulnerability"). Reports in English or Japanese are welcome.

  • Acknowledgement: we aim to reply within 3 business days
  • After a fix, with the reporter's consent, the vulnerability is published as a GitHub Security Advisory
  • Good-faith security research is welcome, but please don't access other users' data, disrupt the service or use social engineering

Only the latest version on the main branch is supported.
