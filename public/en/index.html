A scheduling bot for Discord

Find a time that works,
right in Discord.

Start a scheduling poll with the members of a channel or role already added as participants. From entering date options to answering, confirming and creating a Discord event, no sign-up for any outside service is needed.

Features

What it does

Designed so that both organizers and participants know what to do. It also works from the Discord app on your phone.

Participants set automatically

The members of the channel where you run the command, or of a role you choose, are added as participants from the start. You can also choose them by hand.

Enter date options in bulk

Pick several dates on the calendar and apply a time to all of them at once. Ten options take only a few taps.

Answer by tapping ○△×

A button on the poll message opens your own answer page. No login or account needed.

Turn the result into an event

When the organizer confirms a date and presses [Announce the dates], an announcement is posted to the channel. The date can also be added as a Discord event.

How it works

How it works

Scheduling takes four steps.

  1. 1Organizer

    Create a poll

    Run /schedule (or /di) in a channel. Use the link shown only to you to set the title and participants.

  2. 2Organizer

    Add date options

    Pick the date options on the same page and publish. A poll message is posted to the channel.

  3. 3Participants

    Answer

    Press [Answer] on the poll message, choose ○, △ or × for each option and save. You can also see the other participants' answers.

  4. 4Organizer

    Confirm the date

    Choose a date on the results page and confirm it. You can also remind people who haven't answered and create a Discord event from there.

For admins

For server admins

The bot asks only for the permissions it needs. You can choose the channels where the commands work with Discord's built-in settings.

Permissions given to the bot

PermissionTypeUsed for
View ChannelRequiredChecking the channel it posts to
Send MessagesRequiredPosting the poll and announcements
Embed LinksRequiredShowing the options and counts clearly
Create EventsOptionalAdding the confirmed date as a Discord event
Manage EventsOptionalEditing and deleting events the bot created

The bot does not ask for the Administrator permission. Scheduling still works if you add it without the event permissions.

Choosing where the commands work

  1. Open Server Settings → Integrations → DIscheduler.
  2. Under Channels, turn off All Channels and add the channels where the commands should work.
  3. Leave Roles & Members as it is (@everyone).
  4. Who can use the commands in a channel is controlled by that channel's Send Messages and Use Application Commands permissions.

Members with the Manage Server permission can set defaults for how participants are chosen (channel members, role members or manual) and for the Discord event location with [Server defaults] in /schedule.

Right after the bot is added, anyone who can send messages can use the commands in every channel. To limit where they work, follow the steps above after adding it. To prevent abuse, the number of polls is limited (10 per person per hour, 100 per server per day, and 20 active polls per channel).

Security & privacy

Security and privacy

DIscheduler handles personal information (people's schedules), so security comes first in its design. All of its safeguards are public.

What this bot does not do

  • Ask for your Discord password or a login
  • Ask for the Administrator permission
  • Read your messages
  • Store email addresses or IP addresses
  • Mention @here. It mentions the participants' role (or @everyone without one) only when a poll is published, when dates are announced and in event reminders
  • Load outside analytics or ads
  • Load outside analytics. Ads are shown only on this page, the security policy and the answer-saved page, in a frame isolated from the site (you can decline them)

How answers and data are handled

  • Answers are shared only among the participants of that poll
  • Non-participants can't see them, even with the URL
  • The channel shows only the counts for each option
  • Answer links are shown only to you, valid for 10 minutes and single use
  • Data is deleted automatically 6 months after the deadline or confirmation
  • Organizers can delete a poll at any time

FAQ

FAQ

For other questions, see also the security policy.

Does it cost anything?

No. It runs only on Cloudflare's free tier. If you'd like to help with running costs, see Support (optional).

Can it become unavailable?

Yes. On busy days that go over Cloudflare's free tier (the daily limits on requests and database reads and writes), it stops working for the rest of the day. The free tier resets every day at 00:00 UTC, and it works again from then. Going over the free tier costs nothing, and answers already entered are not lost. If this keeps happening, we plan to move to a paid plan. Please consider supporting us.

Do participants need to log in or sign up?

No. Pressing [Answer] on the poll message sends you an answer link in a message only you can see. Open the link and answer right away.

Can I change my answer later?

Yes. You can change it at any time while the poll exists, even after the deadline or after a date is confirmed. Open the answer page from [Answer] on the poll message. If you change your answer after a date is confirmed, the organizer's results page shows the change.

Who can see my answers?

The participants and the organizer of that poll. To find a time that works, who can make which date is shared among the participants. People who aren't participants, and people outside the server, can't see them.

Does it work with members in other time zones?

Yes. Dates and times are shown in the time zone of the viewer's browser. If the browser's time zone can't be determined, Japan time is used. You can also switch the time zone on the page.

When is the data deleted?

Automatically, 6 months after the deadline or the confirmed date. Organizers can delete a poll earlier at any time. If the bot is removed from a server, that server's data is deleted too.

I found a security issue. What should I do?

Please don't post it publicly. Report it through the contact in "Reporting a vulnerability" in the security policy.
